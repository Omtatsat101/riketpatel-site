Dear Humin team,

I am writing about the Growth Marketing Manager, Lifecycle and MarTech role, and I want to start with a piece of context that is not on my résumé. I am a Healthy Minds Program app user. The app has stayed in my regular rotation since. So this application is not a career switch I am trying on. It is a chance to put my hands on the inside of a product I already trust.

The professional case is straightforward. Seven years at Merck running product programs for clinical-data systems used by teams in six countries. I led the Scale IT for Clinical Portfolio program, which removed roughly 10,000 hours of annual manual effort across four interconnected systems. I designed and shipped the A and R Submission Translation platform, which cut the China NDA cycle from about six months to about a week, through direct user research with stakeholders in Tokyo, Shanghai, Berlin, Brussels, and Boston. I was promoted to Squad Lead in 2022. I ran sprint cadence, mentorship, and audit readiness across three inspection-sensitive systems with zero findings.

The growth marketing experience is more recent and more independent. For the past two years I have been building the full lifecycle and acquisition stack for my own brand portfolio. KiddieSketch is the live revenue brand on Shopify, currently running active Klaviyo lifecycle programs (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back) and Meta and Google Ads campaigns. The combined Kiddie Brands email list is approximately 9,000 subscribers concentrated on KiddieSketch today. KiddieGo and KiddieWordle are the two brands in the June launch wave, with the same flow architecture and ad infrastructure already wired up and ready to deploy. I personally write the Klaviyo flows, run the paid acquisition, manage organic social across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn, write the SEO briefs, and own GA4 and Google Tag Manager. The kind of detail that lives in this work, to give you one example: the KiddieSketch program segments customers by gift-giver versus end-recipient based on shipping address differing from billing address. A grandmother buying a print for her grandson reads differently from a parent buying for their own child, and the email program reflects that. I have not managed an eight-figure marketing budget. I have run small-business marketing where every dollar is mine, which is a different kind of discipline.

What I would like to bring to Humin specifically is twofold. First, given access to the right tools and context, I would help automate the parts of lifecycle and marketing operations that should not need a human in the loop. Behaviorally triggered journeys that route themselves based on app-usage signals. Segmentation that updates without somebody rebuilding the audience every quarter. Reporting the team can trust because the pipeline is clean, not because someone heroically refreshed the dashboard at 11pm. Most of the growth pain I see in mission-driven organizations is the same: smart people doing repetitive lifecycle work by hand because the systems were never built to scale with them. I would build those systems for you over time.

Second, I believe I can help elevate the Humin brand. Healthy Minds has already shifted how people in my own circle talk about meditation, from a vibes-based practice into a science-backed tool. There is real growth on the table from telling that story more confidently across more channels, and from designing lifecycle programs that meet users at the moments they actually need the practice. I have the product instincts, the growth marketing chops, and a personal relationship with the work. Combining all three at one organization is not something I expect to find twice.
