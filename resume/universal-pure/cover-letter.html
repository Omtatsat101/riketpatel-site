At Merck, I led the Scale IT for Clinical Portfolio program: four interconnected clinical-data systems, fifteen-plus stakeholder groups across six countries. On the brokerage side, I now run deal flow for Metta Legacy Partners — listings, financial review, buyer-seller alignment, the kind of multi-stakeholder commercial-and-operational quarterbacking the job posting describes. I've closed adjacent-industry gaps at Merck more than once and I close them faster than most.

Newark is about 45 minutes from my home in Edgewater Park, NJ — close enough to be in your office when it matters. 50% travel including weekends/holidays works for me; I've spent the last seven years on calls into Tokyo at 9pm and into Brussels at 6am, so a flexible cadence isn't new.
