Dear Heather and the Office of Innovation team,

I am writing about the Product Manager role. I live in Edgewater Park, just outside Trenton, so the residents the Office is building services for include my neighbors. I spent seven years at Merck owning the product roadmap for clinical-data systems used by teams in six countries, the kind of regulated, audit-sensitive, multi-stakeholder work where shipping the wrong thing has real consequences for real people. I left Merck in November 2025 to run a small New Jersey real-estate and brokerage practice full-time, and to build a handful of free public-good sites on the side. This role is the first I have seen where all of that lines up in one job description.

At Merck I led the Scale IT for Clinical Portfolio program, which removed roughly 10,000 hours of annual manual effort across four interconnected clinical-data systems and is on track to double. I designed and shipped the A and R Submission Translation platform, which cut translated Clinical Study Reports and supporting NDA documents from 6-7 months to about one month, accelerating regulatory filings to China's NMPA. The research behind it was hands-on user work with stakeholders in Tokyo, Shanghai, Berlin, Brussels, and Boston. I was promoted to Squad Lead in 2022 and ran sprint cadence, mentorship, and audit readiness across three inspection-sensitive systems with zero findings. As Total Cost of Ownership Data Steward I owned the Apptio and Ariba commercial reviews, which is the same build-vs-buy and price-vs-rate-card analysis the role's "evaluate custom vs. SaaS options" requirement is asking for.

I am a first-generation Indian-American. My family came from a small village in Anand, Gujarat. At his farewell event there my father made a public promise to always give back to that village, and I am continuing that promise by setting up a Python and generative-AI teacher position there so the kids growing up where my parents grew up have access to the same tools the rest of the world is learning. The same instinct, that useful things should be reachable by people who cannot pay for them, is why I built KiddieWordle as a word game for kids in seventeen languages with no ads in the play area, BestPythonCourse as plain-English Python guides instead of $300 upsells, and the free rate-finder sites for people trying to understand a mortgage without being routed straight into a sales call. None of those will ever make anyone rich. That is the point. New Jersey's Office of Innovation is the first government team I have seen that operates on that same instinct at scale, reducing unemployment-insurance application time, simplifying small-business filings, getting millions of residents connected to programs they are already entitled to. I would like to spend my next chapter inside that work.

I navigate life with ADHD and dyslexia, so I bring a lived-experience lens to cognitive load, plain language, and what "easy enough" actually means for a stressed person filing paperwork at 11pm. I am also a Certified Mental Health First Aider since 2024. At Merck I was an active member of the capABILITY Network for disability inclusion, the Interfaith Organization, and the Next Gen Network employee resource groups. Equity and accessibility from inception is not a phrase I had to learn for this application. It is already how I evaluate a release.

I live in Edgewater Park, NJ (08010), about an hour to Trenton, comfortably in-office or hybrid. My most recent title was Product Manager at Merck CRWEG, Clinical Trial Analysis and Reporting. Thank you for the work the Office is doing for the state.
