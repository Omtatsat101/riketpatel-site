6. Questions for them

Pick 2-3 per round, never 7. Sharp questions are half the signal.

For recruiter

  • What does "success at six months" look like in concrete metrics?
  • Is this a backfill or a new role? If backfill, what happened?
  • Where's the team strongest, and where's the biggest gap?

For hiring manager

  • What's the biggest current pain in this area?
  • What tools are mid-flight that you'd want this hire to push forward or sunset?
  • What's the unwritten rule of this team that a new hire should know on day one?

For peer interviewers

  • What's the team's biggest constraint right now — headcount, tools, leadership air-cover?
  • Who's the easiest person to collaborate with here, and who's the hardest?

For executive interviewers

  • What's the three-year mission roadmap and where does this role show up in it?
  • If this hire goes well, what does {{COMPANY}} look like in two years that it doesn't today?
