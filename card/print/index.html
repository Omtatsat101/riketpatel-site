How to print at home
  1. Click Print sheet (or open the PDF). You'll get 10 identical cards on one US-Letter page.
  2. In the print dialog set Scale / Size = 100% (not "Fit"), and margins None / Default.
  3. Print on cardstock or Avery 5371 / 8371 / 8871 perforated business-card sheets, then cut.
For a print shop: send them the PDF, or ask for 3.5″ × 2″ with a QR — it's vector, so it stays sharp at any size.

Walgreens / photo prints: use Download 4×6 photo — it's a 1800×1200 (300 DPI) landscape image, the exact 2:3 ratio a 4×6 print needs. Upload it as a standard 4″ × 6″ Glossy photo; the QR is verified to scan from the print.
